About

Human factors specialist with a systems mindset

I’m a Human Factors Specialist with a degree in Industrial Engineering, and my work has always focused ensuring safety and accessibility is a paramount concern. I spent two years in nuclear and four years in rail and transit, where I worked to improve how systems support people rather than create friction, confusion, or risk.

My focus has always been on making sure the system is safe and usable for the people who rely on it, not the other way around. I’m interested in the intersection of human performance, system design, and operational risk, helping organizations create work environments that are easier to understand, safer to use, and more effective in practice.

I care about systems that help people work confidently, not just efficiently.

Always happy to talk research, AI products, or rail.
